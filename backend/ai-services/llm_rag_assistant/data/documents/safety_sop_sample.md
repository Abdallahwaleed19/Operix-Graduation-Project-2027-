# Operix Industrial Facility Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## Document ID: SOP-SAFE-2026-01
**Facility Scope:** Heavy Manufacturing & Automated Welding Cells  
**Status:** Approved Operational Regulation  

### Section 1: Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Requirements
1. Every operator entering the Welding Zone must wear ANSI Z87.1 approved impact-resistant safety goggles.
2. Fire-retardant leather aprons and welding gloves are mandatory during active arc welding operations.
3. Steel-toed boots must be worn in all areas where heavy machinery, overhead cranes, or transport forklifts are operating.
4. Failure to comply with PPE rules will result in immediate system incident flagging and supervisor notification.

### Section 2: Restricted Monitoring Zones
1. The Welding Restricted Zone (Zone ID: eeeeeeee-0001-0001-0001-000000000001) is strictly designated for automated robotic welders.
2. Human entry is prohibited during active welding cycles without lockout/tagout (LOTO) key interlock engagement.
3. If an unauthorized person is detected within the perimeter polygon, the AI vision system immediately raises a Critical severity intrusion incident.

### Section 3: Camera Telemetry & Blackout Reporting
1. Cameras must maintain 99.5% continuous streaming uptime.
2. Any unobserved outage gap exceeding 15 minutes triggers an alert to plant maintenance personnel.
